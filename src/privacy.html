Privacy Policy

Our commitment to privacy

auuki.com ("us", "we", or "our") operates the auuki.com website (hereinafter referred to as the "Service").

Auuki.com is a privacy-first, local-first service. Workout files, settings, and recorded activities are stored in your browser and can be downloaded or deleted by you. The app does not require an Auuki account for normal computer-based training.

What information we collect

Core training workflows do not require you to provide personal information to Auuki. If you connect an optional third-party service, that service may process data according to its own policies.

We do not rent, buy or sell personal data from or to others, use automated decisions or profiling in the processing of your personal data, or process any special category data as per the GDPR Article 9.

Computer hardware access

If you use hardware features, the browser may request permission to access Bluetooth or serial devices connected to your computer. You can change those permissions in your browser or operating system settings.

How we use cookies

Auuki may use browser storage to keep local app settings, workouts, and activity data available across page refreshes.

Data storage

Most app data is stored locally in your browser. Data is only sent outside your computer when you explicitly export it or use an optional third-party connection.

Data retention

Local browser data remains on your computer until you delete it in the app or clear site/browser storage.

When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will delete such information, or, if this is not possible (for example, because your personal information has been stored in backup archives), then we will securely store your personal information and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible.

Whom we share your personal data with

To run our business efficiently and securely, we sometimes will have to share your personal data with other (trusted) parties such as:

IT support, when necessary Public authorities: when we are obliged to report to them We require that all such recipients secure data in accordance with good information security and as per the requirements of this Privacy notice.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies or practices of any third party sites or services.

Links to Other Sites

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click a third party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.
